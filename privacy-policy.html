Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Your privacy and online safety are paramount to our operations.

1. Introduction

At Fireboy and Watergirl Game ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible via https://fireboyandwatergirlgame.github.io/, we respect the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by our platform and how we utilize it.

2. Information We Collect

We prioritize minimal data footprint. We do not require account registration, usernames, passwords, or credit card details to play our games. The data collected consists of:

  • Standard Log Files: Like most web servers, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) automatically logs visitor information including IP addresses, browser user-agents, Internet Service Providers (ISP), referring/exit pages, date/time stamps, and page interactions. This information is non-personally identifiable and used solely for security and performance optimization.
  • Local Storage Data: Games use browser LocalStorage or IndexedDB purely on your local machine to save game progress, high scores, and sound volume preferences.

3. Cookies & Web Beacons

Fireboy and Watergirl Game does not deploy first-party profiling cookies. However, third-party services that support our platform may utilize cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Collects anonymous statistical data regarding visitor behavior and popular games to help us improve site navigation.
  • Third-Party Ad Networks: Advertising partners (such as Google AdSense and GameDistribution) may deploy cookies to deliver relevant advertisements based on general browsing trends.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites.

4. Third-Party Game Embeds & SDKs

Certain games hosted or embedded via iframes (such as GameDistribution or Poki) may run their own client-side SDKs to load game assets and serve programmatic advertisements. These third-party vendors operate under their own privacy policies:

  • GameDistribution Privacy Policy
  • Google Ad & Content Network Privacy Policy

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Protecting the safety of children online is especially important to us. Fireboy and Watergirl Game does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information (PII) from children under the age of 13.

All featured games are curated to ensure they are free from inappropriate adult themes, graphic depictions, or unmoderated chat platforms. If a parent or guardian believes that our website inadvertently possesses information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at contact@funnyshooter2.github.io, and we will promptly erase such records.

6. GDPR & CCPA Data Protection Rights

Visitors from the European Union (under GDPR) and California (under CCPA) are entitled to specific data protection rights, including the right to request access, rectification, erasure, and restriction of data processing. Because our website operates without user accounts or personal profiles, we do not store personal profiles. For any privacy requests, please contact our Data Representative at contact@funnyshooter2.github.io.

7. Consent & Updates

By accessing and playing games on our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. We reserve the right to revise this policy periodically; updates will be reflected directly on this page with an updated timestamp.
