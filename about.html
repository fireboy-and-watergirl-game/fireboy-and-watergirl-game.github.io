🔍 Our Game Testing & Curation Methodology

Not all web games make the cut. Before any game is featured on our portal, it undergoes a rigorous 4-step quality audit:

  1. Device Compatibility Check: We test each title on entry-level Chromebooks, standard laptops, and desktop computers across Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox.
  2. Performance Benchmarking: The game must sustain a steady 50-60 FPS without memory leaks or browser crashes during intense gameplay.
  3. Content Suitability: We verify that content is free from graphic violence or harmful material, maintaining a fun and lighthearted tone suitable for general audiences.
  4. Network & Privacy Audit: We inspect outbound network requests to ensure no third-party trackers violate user privacy or collect unauthorized personal data.
