Fireboy and Watergirl Game is a fun and thrilling 3D first-person shooter (FPS) game where you battle hordes of absurd, comical enemies! Use various weapons to slay these bizarre beings, earn gold, and upgrade your weapons arsenal as you progress. Immerse yourself in this colorful, action-packed world!

About Fireboy and Watergirl Game

Created by developer GoGoMan, Fireboy and Watergirl Game delivers high-energy FPS gameplay packed with humor. Enter surreal arenas filled with goofy redmen, giants, and bizarre creatures. Between waves, visit the in-game shop to unlock over 10 different weapons, equip silencers, holographic scopes, and slap flashy skins on your firearms.

How to Play & Controls

Stay on the move and wipe out all enemies in each round to advance to the next challenge!

Keyboard & Mouse Controls

  • W / A / S / D: Move character
  • Mouse: Look around and aim
  • Left Click: Fire weapon
  • Right Click: Aim down sights (Zoom)
  • Space: Jump
  • Left Shift: Sprint
  • 1 - 9 / Scroll Wheel: Switch weapons
  • R: Reload ammo
  • G: Throw grenade

Pro Tips & Strategy

  • Never stop moving: Circle-strafe around enemy hordes to prevent being cornered.
  • Aim for the head: Headshots deal massive multiplier damage to clear tough enemies quickly.
  • Save explosives for swarms: Use RPGs and grenades on clustered groups and large bosses.
  • Upgrade wisely: Focus on improving damage, magazine size, and reload speed on your favorite assault rifle first.
