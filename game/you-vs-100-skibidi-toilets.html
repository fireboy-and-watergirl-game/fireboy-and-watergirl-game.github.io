You vs 100 Skibidi Toilets pits you alone against an army of 100 hilarious singing toilet monsters! Unleash devastating heavy firepower to survive the onslaught.

About You vs 100 Skibidi Toilets

You vs 100 Skibidi Toilets is an action-packed 3D wave survival shooter. Fight through huge swarms of bizarre toilet creatures, unlock mini-guns and rocket launchers, and defeat giant toilet bosses.

How to Play

Backpedal and keep moving to stay out of biting range, fire automatic weapons into clusters of toilet monsters, pick up dropped ammo, and survive 100 waves.

Controls

  • WASD: Move
  • Mouse: Aim & Shoot
  • Space: Jump
  • Shift: Sprint
  • 1-6: Switch Weapon
