Airport Clash 3D

★★★★★ 78 votes

Airport Clash 3D puts you in command of a ruthless raider squad fighting to seize an abandoned airport from rival forces. Armed with a heavy Gatling gun, lead your team to victory in furious firefights.

About Airport Clash 3D

Airport Clash 3D delivers action-packed third-person multiplayer shooting gameplay. Upgrade your health and weapon damage between matches as you strive for dominance across the hangar and runway arenas.

How to Play

Storm enemy spawn zones, collect medkits to stay alive, eliminate rival gang members, and capture the airfield to earn points for your squad.

Controls

  • WASD: Move
  • Mouse: Aim & Shoot
  • Space: Jump
  • C: Crouch
