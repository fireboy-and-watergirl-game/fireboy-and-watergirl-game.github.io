Cat Gunner vs Zombies is an action-packed 2D shooter where a brave feline warrior takes on hordes of mutated zombie creatures to protect the town.

About Cat Gunner vs Zombies

Cat Gunner features explosive side-scrolling shoot-em-up action. Upgrade your feline arsenal with shotguns, machine guns, and laser cannons while rescuing trapped kitten survivors.

How to Play

Move through infected streets, blast oncoming zombie cats, collect fish coins to purchase upgrades, and defeat gigantic mutant boss beasts.

Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Move & Jump
  • Mouse / Space: Aim & Shoot
  • 1-4: Switch Weapons
