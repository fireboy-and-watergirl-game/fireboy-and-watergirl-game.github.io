Raft Wars is a beloved classic turn-based artillery shooter. Help baby Simon and his brother protect their precious buried treasure from greedy pirates!

About Raft Wars

Raft Wars combines charming humor with trajectory calculation. Aim your tennis ball launcher, calculate wind and arc, and knock pirate ships and raiders into the ocean.

How to Play

Adjust trajectory angle and launch power with your cursor. Hit enemy rafts to knock enemies overboard, earn coins, and upgrade your raft with rockets and armor.
