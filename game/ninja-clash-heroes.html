Ninja Clash Heroes

★★★★★ 88 votes

Ninja Clash Heroes is a stylish 3D team shooter set in ancient Japanese pagodas. Choose your ninja character and fight for control of the sacred Golden Cat!

About Ninja Clash Heroes

Ninja Clash Heroes features distinct warrior classes including the sneaky Scout, heavy Guard, lethal Sniper, and supportive Geisha. Level up your heroes and upgrade your weapons.

How to Play

Team up with allies, navigate multi-level garden shrines, unleash devastating character abilities, and capture the objective to secure team victory.

Controls

  • WASD: Move
  • Mouse: Aim & Shoot
  • Space: Jump
  • C: Crouch
  • G: Grenade
